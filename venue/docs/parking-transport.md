# Parking and transportation

## On-site parking
Free on-site parking for up to 120 cars. Overnight parking is permitted, and cars must be picked up by 10:00 am the next day.

## Overflow parking
For larger events, overflow parking is available at the Alder Springs Community Church lot, a five-minute walk away, by arrangement with your coordinator.

## Shuttles and rideshare
Shuttles and buses may drop off at the front circle. The rideshare pickup point is the front circle as well. We recommend arranging transportation for guests who will be drinking.
