# Decor rules

## What's welcome
You are welcome to decorate with flowers, table decor, signage, draping on our installed hooks, and your own lighting on battery or standard outlets.

## Candles
Candles must be fully enclosed in glass, with the flame below the rim. Unenclosed candles and floating lanterns are not permitted.

## Not permitted
- Confetti, glitter, rice and birdseed, indoors or out
- Nails, tacks, tape or adhesive on walls, beams or woodwork
- Fog or haze machines (they set off our fire detectors)

## Petal toss
Real flower petals are permitted on the Garden Terrace only.

## Cleanup
Our staff resets tables and chairs. You or your planner are responsible for collecting your decor by the end of the event.
