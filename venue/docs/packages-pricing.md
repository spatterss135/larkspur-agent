# Packages and pricing

Every event has three parts: the room rental, a catering package, and any add-ons. Your coordinator prepares an itemized quote for your date, guest count and package.

## Room rental
Rental rates depend on the room, the day of the week and the season. Peak season is May through October. Grand Hall rental starts at $3,500 for a Monday–Thursday evening in the off-season. Saturdays in peak season are our highest rate. Rental rates and food and beverage minimums are the same for daytime and evening events. Ask us for a quote for your specific date.

## Catering packages
All food and beverage is provided by our in-house kitchen. Packages are priced per guest:
- **Classic** ($95 per guest): passed appetizers, a plated or buffet dinner with two entrées, house wine and beer service for four hours, coffee and tea.
- **Signature** ($135 per guest): everything in Classic, plus a stationed appetizer display, a three-course plated dinner with a choice of three entrées, a premium bar for five hours and a champagne toast.

## Food and beverage minimum
Each room and date has a food and beverage minimum. If your catering total comes in under the minimum, the difference is added to your bill as a minimum adjustment. Minimums are highest on Saturdays in peak season.

## Add-ons
- Late-night snack: $12 per guest
- Ceremony on the Garden Terrace (May–October): $1,500
- Extra hour of event time: $750, for evening events in the Grand Hall or the Library Room only (see "Noise and end time")

## Service fee and tax
A 22% service fee applies to food and beverage, including any minimum adjustment. Sales tax of 8.5% applies to the full bill, including rental, add-ons and the service fee.
