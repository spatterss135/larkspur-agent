# Outside vendors and our preferred list

## Bringing your own vendors
You may bring your own photographer, florist, DJ or band, officiant, hair and makeup artist, and planner. Every outside vendor must provide a certificate of liability insurance to your coordinator at least 14 days before your event.

## Preferred vendors
These vendors know the building well. We don't take commissions from any of them.
- Photography: Fieldnote Studio, Aspen & Ash Photo
- Florals: Wild Thistle Floral
- Music: The Creekside Strings (ceremony), DJ Marisol Vega (reception)
- Rentals: High Plains Party Rental

## Load-in
Vendors may load in through the north service door, starting at your setup time. Vendors have one hour after your end time to break down. Items that can't go out that night may be picked up by 10:00 am the next morning, by arrangement.
