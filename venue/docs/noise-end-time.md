# Noise and end time

## End time
Evening events end at 11:00 pm, and guests should be off the property by 11:30 pm. Daytime events end at 3:00 pm.

## Amplified music
Amplified music on the Garden Terrace must end at 10:00 pm, because of our neighbors across the creek. Amplified music inside the Grand Hall may continue until your event's end time, with the doors closed. The Library Room allows background music only (no DJ or band), because it can host a separate event on the same day.

## Extra hours
One extra hour is available for evening events in the Grand Hall or the Library Room, ending no later than midnight. Daytime events and Garden Terrace events cannot be extended.

When you add an extra hour, everything moves with your end time:
- your event ends one hour later (for example, midnight instead of 11:00 pm),
- bar service ends 30 minutes before the new end time,
- amplified music in the Grand Hall may continue until the new end time,
- guests should be off the property 30 minutes after the new end time.

## Breakdown
Your vendors have one hour after your end time to break down and load out.
