# Weather and the terrace backup plan

## Rain plan
Every Garden Terrace event includes the Grand Hall as an indoor backup at no extra charge. On the day before your event, your coordinator will check the forecast with you, and we make the final call together by noon on the event day.

## Cool evenings and wind
Heaters are available at no charge for cool evenings. In high wind, we may take down string lighting and tall decor for safety.

## Off-season
The Garden Terrace is closed from November through April. Events in those months are held indoors.
