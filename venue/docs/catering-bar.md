# Catering and bar

## In-house catering
Our kitchen caters every event at Larkspur Hall. Outside caterers are not permitted, with two exceptions: your wedding cake or desserts from a licensed bakery, and cultural or religious meals by arrangement with your coordinator.

## Dietary needs
We accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and common allergy needs at no extra charge. Please list dietary needs with your final guest count.

## Tasting
Couples booking the Signature package receive a complimentary tasting for two after signing their contract. Classic package tastings are available for $150.

## Bar service
All alcohol is served by our licensed bartenders. Outside alcohol is not permitted. We stop bar service 30 minutes before your event's end time, and we do not serve shots. Guests must be 21 or older to be served, and our staff may ask for ID.

## Cake cutting
Bakery cakes are welcome. We cut and plate them at no charge.
