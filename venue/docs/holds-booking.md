# Holds and how booking works

## What a hold is
A hold sets a date aside for you for 48 hours while you decide. A hold is free, and it is not a booking. When the 48 hours end, the date becomes available to others again.

## Hold rules
- One active hold per couple or host at a time
- Holds last 48 hours and cannot be extended
- High-demand dates (such as Saturdays in September and October, and New Year's Eve) may not be eligible for holds. A coordinator decides each request.

## How booking works
1. Check availability for your date.
2. Optionally place a hold while you decide.
3. Your coordinator sends an itemized quote and the event contract.
4. Your date is reserved once the contract is signed and the deposit is paid.

Only a coordinator can confirm a booking.
