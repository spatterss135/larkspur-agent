# Larkspur Hall: overview and rooms

Larkspur Hall is a restored 1920s grange hall in Alder Springs, Colorado, hosting weddings, rehearsal dinners, milestone birthdays and company events. We host one main event per day, so the venue is yours for the evening.

## Grand Hall
Our main room, with exposed timber trusses, a hardwood dance floor and a built-in stage.
- Seated dinner: up to 200 guests
- Standing reception: up to 250 guests
- Includes round tables, chairs, house linens, a dance floor and the house sound system

## Garden Terrace
A stone terrace overlooking the creek, open **May through October only**.
- Seated dinner: up to 120 guests
- Standing reception: up to 150 guests
- String lighting included. Heaters are available on request at no charge.
- The terrace is also available for ceremonies before a reception in the Grand Hall.

## Library Room
A wood-paneled private room for rehearsal dinners, showers and small celebrations.
- Seated dinner: up to 40 guests
- Standing reception: up to 60 guests

## Event times
Evening events run from 5:00 pm to 11:00 pm. Daytime events run from 10:00 am to 3:00 pm. Your rental includes two hours of setup time before your start time.

## Getting in touch
Our event coordinators are in the office Tuesday through Saturday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. Email events@larkspurhall.example.
