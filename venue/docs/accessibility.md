# Accessibility

## Getting in and around
The main entrance has a ramp from the front circle. The Grand Hall, the Library Room and the restrooms are on one level. The Garden Terrace is reached by a gently sloped path from the Grand Hall.

## Restrooms
Accessible restrooms are located off the main lobby.

## Parking
Four accessible parking spaces are next to the main entrance.

## Other needs
If you or your guests have accessibility needs we haven't covered here, let your coordinator know and we will do our best to accommodate them.
