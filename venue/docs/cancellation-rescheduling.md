# Cancellation and rescheduling

## Cancelling your event
Cancellations must be made in writing to your coordinator. What we keep depends on how far ahead you cancel:
- **More than 180 days before your event:** we keep your deposit (30% of your estimated total). Any payments above that are refunded.
- **90 to 180 days before your event:** we keep 50% of your estimated total. If you've paid more than that, we refund the difference. If you've paid less, the difference is due.
- **Fewer than 90 days before your event:** we keep 100% of your estimated total, and any unpaid balance is due.

## Rescheduling
You may move your event once, to any available date within 12 months of the original date, at no charge, if you request the change more than 90 days before your event. Payments already made carry over to the new date. If the new date has a higher rental rate or food and beverage minimum, the difference applies.

A change requested 90 days or less before your event is handled as a cancellation under the policy above.

## Severe weather and emergencies
If local authorities close roads or prohibit gatherings on your event date, we will work with you to reschedule within 12 months without penalty.
