# Cancellation and rescheduling

## Cancelling your event
Cancellations must be made in writing to your coordinator.
- More than 180 days before your event: you forfeit your deposit.
- 90 to 180 days before your event: 50% of your estimated total is due.
- Fewer than 90 days before your event: 100% of your estimated total is due.

## Rescheduling
You may move your event once, to any available date within 12 months of the original date, at no charge if you request the change more than 90 days in advance. Payments already made carry over to the new date. If the new date has a higher rental rate or minimum, the difference applies.

## Severe weather and emergencies
If local authorities close roads or prohibit gatherings on your event date, we will work with you to reschedule within 12 months without penalty.
