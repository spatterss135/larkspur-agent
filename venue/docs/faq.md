# Frequently asked questions

## Can we have our ceremony and reception at Larkspur Hall?
Yes. Many couples hold the ceremony on the Garden Terrace (May through October) and the reception in the Grand Hall.

## Do you have a minimum guest count?
There's no minimum guest count, but each room and date has a food and beverage minimum. See "Packages and pricing".

## Is there a getting-ready space?
Yes. For evening events in the Grand Hall, the Library Room is yours as a getting-ready suite from 11:00 am on the day of your event.

## Do you have Wi-Fi?
Yes, guest Wi-Fi is available throughout the building.

## Can we see the venue before deciding?
Yes, we offer tours Tuesday through Saturday. See "Tours".

## Do you offer discounts?
Our published rates apply to all events. If you have questions about pricing, your coordinator is happy to talk through options for your date.

## Can kids attend?
Yes, children are welcome, and high chairs are available. Ask your coordinator about kids' meal options.
