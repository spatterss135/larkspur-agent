# Tours

## When we offer tours
We offer tours Tuesday through Saturday, between 10:00 am and 4:00 pm. Each tour takes about 45 minutes and is led by one of our coordinators.

## Booking a tour
You can request a tour by email. We can schedule one upcoming tour per couple or host at a time. If you need to change the time, just let us know.

## Tours on event days
On Saturdays with an event, tours may be limited to the Library Room and the terrace.

## What to bring
Bring your guest count estimate and any dates you're considering. We can check availability during the tour.
